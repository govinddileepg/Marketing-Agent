# Product Direction

## Problem

Small businesses often know their products but do not have a repeatable system for turning that knowledge into consistent, platform-appropriate marketing.

## Product

Marketing Agent is an AI marketing operating system. It builds a structured understanding of a business, turns that understanding into a visibility strategy and content calendar, creates content from approved business assets, and eventually publishes and learns from performance.

## V1 workflow

1. Create a business profile.
2. Add business/product information and real assets.
3. Run Business Analysis.
4. Review the generated Marketing Profile.
5. Generate a 30-day Visibility Plan.
6. Select a content idea.
7. Generate an Instagram creative using supplied product assets.
8. Review and approve the creative.
9. Publish through a social publishing integration.

## V1 safety boundary

No content is publicly published automatically. Human approval is required.

## V2 direction

- More platforms and platform-specific adaptation
- Automated scheduling
- Performance analytics
- Campaign-level experimentation
- Feedback-driven content optimization
- Optional autopilot with configurable approval/confidence rules

## Invitepedia pilot

Invitepedia is the first internal pilot. Its real product/invitation samples should be treated as source assets for creative generation. The Marketing Agent must not modify the Invite Studio codebase.
